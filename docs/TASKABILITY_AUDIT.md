# SkillOrbit taskability audit

## Audit

Prerequisite planning combines two selections and transitive graph rules. Eligibility combines versions, completions, expiry, and filters. Assessment combines learner-owned drafts and delayed scoring. Certification combines evidence, permissions, and time transitions. Fixed courses, edges, attempts, learners, and clocks support 1–4-file patches. Copy-only course work is weak; replacing the learning graph or building an LMS is too broad.
